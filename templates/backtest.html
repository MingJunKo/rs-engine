Read this first

Survivorship bias — the big one. The stocks are today's universe: companies that still exist and are liquid in 2026. Stocks that went bust or were delisted over the decade are missing, and some of today's names were tiny or unlisted years ago, so their early "setups" are flattered. Real results over 10 years would be lower; treat the early years with the most suspicion.
Theme gate. Built from the theme ETFs' RS on each day, using today's holdings; many thematic ETFs launched after 2016, so early-year theme rankings use fewer groups.
Execution. Daily bars only: entries at the pivot (or the open if it gaps over), stops assumed hit on the breakout day if the day's low reached them, no slippage or commissions, no taxes. Real fills on fast breakouts are usually worse.
Use it for the shape of the curve, the size and length of drawdowns, and the comparison with buy-and-hold, not as a promise of a return.
