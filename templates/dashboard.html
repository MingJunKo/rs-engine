How to read this

RS Rating (1–99) — volatility-adjusted relative strength (Jeff Sun's VARS idea). Each day's move is measured in ATRs, the benchmark's ATR-moves are subtracted, and the result is summed over 1W, 1M and 3M, ranked across the universe and blended 15/35/50. A quiet stock grinding up beats a wild one that only got there by being volatile. Δ1W = change in rating over 5 sessions.
RS Win % — share of the last 20 sessions the ticker beat the benchmark. 75% = beat it on 15 of 20 days: persistent, not one spike. RS 1M = performance relative to the benchmark over a month. Breadth = % of the ETF's holdings in your universe above their 50-DMA.
Leaders — stocks belonging to the top themes (best ETF per theme), with RS Rating ≥ min, above the 50-DMA, price and $-volume above your floors. TT = Minervini trend template. Ext = more than 4 ATRs above the 50-DMA (don't chase). NH = within 15% of the 52-week high. New = not on the list a week ago.
