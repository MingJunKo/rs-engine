Episodic pivots & parabolic shorts — rules
Episodic pivot — gap ≥ 8% over the prior close, volume ≥ 3× the 50-day average, ≥ $10M traded, price ≥ $3, closing in the top half of the day's range. NEGLECTED = up less than 20% in the 3 months before the gap (Pradeep's best EPs come out of neglect). 9M = 9 million+ shares. HOLDING = still above the gap-day low.
Catalyst — EARNINGS when the gap came within 3 days of a reported quarter; otherwise the latest headlines (Yahoo / Nasdaq). Always read the actual news: an EP needs a real change in the story (earnings/sales surprise, guidance, FDA, contract, M&A).
Pre-market — on US trading days around 8:45am New York time the pre-market job lists stocks gapping ≥ 8% pre-market on real volume, with their headlines, and sends them to Telegram.
Parabolic short — ≥ 50% in 5 days (≥ 25% for stocks over $10B), ≥ 25% above the 10-EMA, $20M+ traded. FIRST RED = today closed red after the run: the classic trigger. Before that, it's a watch, not a short. Size small; these squeeze.
