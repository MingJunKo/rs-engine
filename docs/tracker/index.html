Do the setups work?

Every RS-leader SETUP and every Weekly scan name is logged on the day it appears and followed for 20 sessions, scored the way you'd trade it: buy the break of the pivot, stop under the tight area. The backtest rebuilds the same SETUP shape over the past year so there's a track record from day one.

How it's scored

Entry — the first break above the pivot (the 20-day high on the signal day) within 10 sessions, at the open if it gaps over, otherwise at the pivot. No break in 10 sessions = no trigger (it never set off your buy stop).
Stop — the lowest low of the 10 sessions up to the signal (under the tight area). Hit = stopped, at the open if it gaps below.
Exit — otherwise the close 20 sessions after entry (closed); still inside that window = running.
R — result in multiples of the risk (entry − stop). MFE — the best R reached along the way, i.e. what a good trail could have kept. Hit 2R — % of trades that reached +2R at some point.
This is a fixed, mechanical exit to compare setups fairly; your own selling rules (partial at 2–3R, 10/20-day trail) will differ. The backtest has no top-theme filter (theme history isn't stored) and uses today's universe, so it slightly flatters survivors.
