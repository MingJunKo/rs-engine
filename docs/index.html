Risk appetite

Ratio vs its 50-day average · 6 months

How to read these — 30 seconds
The line is one group divided by another. Rising = the first is beating the second. The dotted line is its 50-day average.
RISK-ON = above its average and higher than a month ago. Anything else is RISK-OFF. The % is the ratio's change over a month.
Read them together. 5+ risk-on = broad appetite: press size. 1 or fewer = defensive tape: smaller and fewer trades. In between = be selective.
For breakouts, watch Momentum, Equal vs Cap and Small vs Large first: they show whether leaders are being rewarded and whether the move is broad. Credit often turns before stocks do.

Minervini trend template — all 8 must pass for the TT flag
  1. Price above the 150-day and 200-day moving averages
  2. 150-day MA above the 200-day MA
  3. 200-day MA trending up for at least 1 month
  4. 50-day MA above both the 150-day and 200-day MAs
  5. Price above the 50-day MA
  6. Price at least 30% above its 52-week low
  7. Price within 25% of its 52-week high
  8. RS rating of 70 or higher (Minervini prefers 80–90+)

Stage 2 uptrend filter from Trade Like a Stock Market Wizard. It says the trend is healthy — not that it's a buy point. Combine with SETUP (tight, near a pivot, not extended) and your own VCP read on the chart.

Column guide
RS — volatility-adjusted RS rating 1–99 vs RSP (1W/1M/3M blended 15/35/50). Δ1W — change over 5 sessions.
RS Win 1M — % of the last 20 sessions it beat RSP. Persistence, not one spike.
Off High — % below the 52-week closing high.
ADR% — average daily range, 20 days. Your floor: ${MIN_ADR}%.
ATR ext — ATRs above the 50-DMA. Jeff avoids new entries above 4 (flag EXT).
Pivot — % below the 20-day high. Within 6% = close to a breakout level.
Tight — 10-day high-low range measured in ADRs. ≤ 2.5 = contracting; lower is tighter.
Vol dry — 10-day ÷ 50-day average volume. Below 1 = volume drying up in the base.
RVol — last session's volume ÷ 50-day average.
ER — next earnings date. Red when within 7 days (no new entries).
SETUP — near pivot (≤ 6%), tight (≤ 2.5 ADR), 0–4 ATR above the 50-DMA. A screen for buyable shape, not a buy signal.
TT trend template · NH within 15% of high · NEW joined the list this week.
CANSLIM — the 7 letters light up when the stock passes: C latest quarter EPS ≥ +25% YoY · A 3-yr EPS growth ≥ 25%/yr · N within 15% of the high · S up-day volume ≥ 1.2× down-day volume (50d) · L RS ≥ 80 · I more funds than last quarter · M most risk gauges risk-on. A faint letter = no data. Context, not a filter: price and RS lead, fundamentals explain.
Funds — number of institutions holding it (SEC 13F, latest filed quarter) and the change vs the quarter before. Rising sponsorship = the "I". Open the chart for the 8-quarter trend.

Early turners

Groups outside the top themes whose RS is turning up · watchlist, not buys

How a group qualifies
Group — not in the top themes, RS ≥ 40 and back above its 20-day, with RS up ≥ 12 in a week or ≥ 8 in 3 days, confirmed by breadth up ≥ 10 pts in a week or an Improving/Leading rotation quadrant.
Stocks — members above their 50-day with RS ≥ 70 and rising, liquid. Sorted with RS-line highs first.
RS FIRST — the RS line is at a 3-month high while price is still more than 5% below its high: the stock is outperforming before it breaks out. Often the earliest tell.
How to use — put them on alert and wait for a proper setup. Many turns fail after a week; the ones that persist usually show up in Leaders within a few weeks.

Leader history

How to read this

RS Rating (1–99) — volatility-adjusted relative strength (Jeff Sun's VARS idea). Each day's move is measured in ATRs, the benchmark's ATR-moves are subtracted, and the result is summed over 1W, 1M and 3M, ranked across the universe and blended 15/35/50. A quiet stock grinding up beats a wild one that only got there by being volatile. Δ1W = change in rating over 5 sessions.
RS Win % — share of the last 20 sessions the ticker beat the benchmark. 75% = beat it on 15 of 20 days: persistent, not one spike. RS 1M = performance relative to the benchmark over a month. Breadth = % of the ETF's holdings in your universe above their 50-DMA.
Leaders — stocks belonging to the top themes (best ETF per theme), with RS Rating ≥ min, above the 50-DMA, price and $-volume above your floors. TT = Minervini trend template. Ext = more than 4 ATRs above the 50-DMA (don't chase). NH = within 15% of the 52-week high. New = not on the list a week ago. SETUP = near a pivot, tight, not extended — the ones to put on your Focus list.
